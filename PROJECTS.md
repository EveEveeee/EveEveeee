# Projects & reusable skills

I focus on defining the problem, shaping the first usable workflow, and making quality and limitations visible. AI coding tools help me implement and verify prototypes. The work below is exploratory; stages are kept separate from production or business-impact claims.

## Product Foundry

**Focus:** turning ideas and documents into scoped requirements, confirmed decisions, linked PRDs, and review evidence.

**My work:** product scope, task flows, interaction rules, and acceptance criteria, with AI-assisted implementation and review.

**Current stage:** three separate pieces—a desktop workbench based on an existing framework, a local requirements-planning web prototype, and an evidence-led requirements-review agent POC. They are not an integrated production service. Full integration and end-to-end experience validation remain to be completed.

[Repository](https://github.com/EveEveeee/product-foundry) · [Product case](https://wangyangjuan.surge.sh/projects/product-foundry.html)

## Muse10 · AI Nails

**Focus:** nail-design co-creation and an in-store journey toward personal-hand try-on.

**My work:** research and competitor analysis, scope and flows, interaction prototypes, prompt iteration, failure cases, and acceptance checks. This is a two-person, co-built exploration.

**Current stage:** an interaction prototype and a separate local backend POC. Initial consumer surveys inform the direction; they do not establish market demand. Real model outputs still show design-completeness issues. Reliable personal-hand try-on, store value, and willingness to pay remain under validation.

[Repository](https://github.com/EveEveeee/muse10-ai-nails) · [Product case](https://wangyangjuan.surge.sh/projects/muse10-ai-nails.html)

## Child-led Knowledge Co-creation

**Focus:** age-aware story creation, where the child makes choices and AI develops a story within those choices.

**My work:** age-specific flows, stage prompts, context boundaries, and quality rules, with AI-assisted prototyping.

**Current stage:** a local story-creation demo covering first drafts, listening, limited revisions, illustrations, and saved storybooks. Earlier tests cover bounded model and function cases. Latest real-microphone end-to-end tests, manual image-quality checks, and family usability sessions are still pending. No educational-outcome claim is made.

[Repository](https://github.com/EveEveeee/child-knowledge-cocreation) · [Product case](https://wangyangjuan.surge.sh/projects/child-knowledge-cocreation.html)

## Evidence-led Product Teardown

**Focus:** investigating a product from accessible first-hand evidence rather than guessing how it works.

**Output:** a traceable HTML report across user, technology, model, and data layers—or an evidence-gap diagnosis when a full analysis is not supported.

**Current stage:** a published reusable skill.

[Skill](https://github.com/EveEveeee/Eve/tree/main/skills/evidence-led-product-teardown) · [Workflow](https://wangyangjuan.surge.sh/tools/product-teardown.html)

**Current stage:** a co-built, reusable skill using fixed canvases, templates, and prompt rules. Catalog hand-model visuals should not be confused with validated personal-hand try-on.
